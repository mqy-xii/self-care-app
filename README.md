# Dopamenu App 🐬🌈✨
### https://mqy-xii.github.io/self-care-app
A dopamenu, or self care menu, attempts to combat burnout by taking more intentional breaks, sprinkling in dopamine throughout the day, and over all tending to self care activities. The Dopamenu app allows you to view and randomize from a list of self care items for that sweet, sweet hit of dopamine.

The selection of items are tailored to me and what "fills **my** cup," but many of the items may be applicable to a wider audience.
